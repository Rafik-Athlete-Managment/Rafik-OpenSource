## How to use the checklist

The boxes below are **per-release/per-installation actions**, not a claim that implemented features are absent. Earlier passing evidence remains in verification; unperformed items stay unchecked. Cloud-provider validation and first-user feedback are accepted backlog. Signing backup/updated physical acceptance apply specifically to Android distribution. Live-data approval and production operations belong to each institution's deployment.
